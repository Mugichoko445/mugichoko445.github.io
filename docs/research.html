Research

I develop visual computing and XR methods that enable people to capture real-world environments, transform them into interactive digital representations, and experience them through immersive technologies.

Capture

Acquiring the real world reliably and accessibly.

Human-Centered Volumetric Capture

Human-centered 3D vision and AR for consistent and comprehensive scene capture.

Focal-Stack Imaging and 3D Reconstruction

Recovering depth and multi-layer scenes from focal stacks, and bringing focus cues to mixed reality.

Specialized Camera Systems and View Synthesis

Multi-camera and mmWave sensing setups for occlusion-free surgical views and through-wall pose reconstruction.

Represent

Transforming observations into interactive 3D content.

3D Scene Understanding and Scene Graphs

Structured 3D scene representations for reasoning about and interacting with the real world.

Visualization in Augmented Reality

Surveys and taxonomies of how visualizations and radiance fields are designed and used in XR.

Diminished Reality and Scene Completion

Removing 3D objects from a live scene for computationally mediated reality.

Neural Scene Representations and Rendering

Learned visibility, lighting, lens, and camera models for efficient and coherent neural rendering.

Experience

Connecting immersive content with perception and action.

Perceptual Modulation in XR

Illusory effects that arise from visual augmentation operating through mediated reality.

VR Locomotion and Movement Perception

How seated, walk-in-place, teleport, and avatar-based locomotion shape movement perception in VR.

Encountered-Type Haptics

Physical feedback that meets the user on demand, such as an extendable pen for drawing above a desk.

XR Guidance and Authoring

AR and VR tutorials, remote assistance, and previsualization tools for guiding tasks and creating content.
